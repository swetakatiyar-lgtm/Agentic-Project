// Problem College students struggle to manage their digital payments because important financial information is scattered across UPI apps, bank accounts, SMS, and purchase histories. They often lose track of refunds, spend time searching for payment proof, get confused about shared expenses, miss recurring subscription deductions, and face uncertainty during failed transactions. This fragmented experience makes everyday money management time-consuming, stressful, and prone to mistakes.
// solution is 
To create an AI financial assistant that automatically organizes payment information from UPI apps, bank SMS, emails, and purchase history into one timeline. Instead of manually checking multiple apps, the AI agent tracks transactions, detects issues, reminds users about pending actions, and prepares the next step when needed
